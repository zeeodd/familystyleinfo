Pitch

Family Style is a frantic mobile co-op party game for 2–8 players where chaos reigns in the kitchen! Race against the clock to pass ingredients from phone to phone and assemble recipes together with your friends. Communicate fast and clearly to overcome wild kitchen hazards and cookware minigames in this ultimate test of culinary teamwork!

Description

Still too many cooks in the kitchen? Perfect. Welcome back to FAMILY STYLE!

The co-op cooking game you love is back, and it’s bigger, tastier, and more chaotic than ever before! Shout louder and scramble faster in this long-awaited sequel to the hit party game for 2 to 8 friends.

Race against the clock to juggle dozens of new ingredients and complete hundreds of new recipes as your team struggles to communicate and stay organized. Achievements, cookware minigames, round hazards, and a new profile system ensure every round feels fresh and unique!

How long can your team keep it together when the kitchen starts fighting back?

🤝 COOPERATIVE

Pass ingredients, call out orders, and keep your kitchen running as a team. Every chef has a role, and clear communication is the difference between success and disaster!

⚡ FRANTIC

Tables fill up, mistakes pile on, and the clock never stops. Think fast or fall behind. Cookware minigames and unexpected hazards will only crank up the chaos!

✨ EXPRESSIVE

Customize your profile, take on unique achievements, and earn rewards every time you play. Whether you’re a master chef or a butterfingers, everyone will know your style.

About the Studio

Co-op Kitchen is an independent game studio that was formed by seven undergraduate seniors. The studio rose to prominence after releasing Family Style, a chaotic, multiplayer mobile cooking game that became an unexpected international viral hit in Thailand, Taiwan, and Vietnam. Building upon the original recipe, the now smaller studio is releasing its long-awaited sequel, Family Style: Second Serving.
